# Cloud Platform Research — Reflection

## What I Learned
Through this research, I gained a clear understanding of AWS, Azure, and GCP. I learned about each provider's basic services, global presence, and what makes each one different. I also practiced finding information from official websites and organizing it into structured documents.

## Comparing the Providers
I found that all three offer similar core services — compute, storage, networking, and databases — but each has its own strengths. AWS is widely used and has the most services available. Azure integrates easily with Microsoft tools many companies already use. GCP stands out for its global network and strong focus on data and AI.

## Challenges
Some concepts were new to me and took time to understand. Navigating each website to find the right information required patience. I also learned that comparing three different platforms means looking at how they describe things, since each one organizes information differently.

## Conclusion
This project gave me a helpful introduction to cloud computing. I now understand the basics of the three major providers and can tell them apart. I also learned how to research and compare services — a skill that will help me as I continue learning more about this field.
